# Getting started

A guide for families, teachers and speech and language therapists setting Saywell up for someone.

## 1. Install and open Saywell

Follow [Install](../README.md#install). Saywell opens on the **Home** board. It already talks: tap
**I**, **want**, **more**, then the message bar at the top.

## 2. Choose a voice

Open **Settings** (press and hold the gear at the top right; the hold stops it being opened by
accident) › **Voice**.

- *Saywell voice* is the built-in, offline voice. More voices, including male voices and other
  languages, are under **Saywell voices** › **Download voices** (about 60 MB each, needs internet
  once). Tap the speaker icon next to a voice to hear it.
- *The phone's voice* uses Android's text-to-speech engine instead.
- **Speaking speed** suits most people a little slower at first.

Many people prefer a voice that matches their age and gender; try a few together.

## 3. Get to know the starter boards

| Board | What's on it |
|---|---|
| Home | Core words for most sentences (*I, you, want, like, more, stop, go, help, eat, drink, play, feel, yes, no, not, all done, please, thank you…*) and folders for the topic boards |
| Chat | Quick phrases: greetings, *I don't understand*, *wait*, *I need help*, *I need a break*, *bathroom*, *it hurts* |
| Feelings, Food & drink, People, Places, Activities, Body | Topic words |
| Questions, Describe, Time | Question words, describing words and colours, time words |
| Letters | Spell words letter by letter (*space* starts a new word, *delete* removes a letter) |

Words are coloured by type (people yellow, actions green, describing blue, things orange, social
pink, questions purple, *no*/*stop* red), as in most AAC systems.

## 4. Make it personal

Press and hold the **pencil** to edit, then:

- tap a button to change its words, picture or colour; tap an empty space to add a button;
- **Say instead** lets a short label say something longer ("hi" → "Hello, nice to see you!");
- pictures can be symbols (search by word), a photo from the gallery, or a new photo — photos of
  family, pets and favourite things are often the most motivating words;
- **Hide while talking** keeps a button's place but hides it until you're ready to teach it;
- press and hold a button, then tap another space, to move it;
- the bar at the top has **Undo**, **Board settings** (name, rows and columns) and **All boards**.

Keep words in the same place once someone has learned them: people remember positions.

## 5. Adjust for how the person moves and sees

**Settings › Touch**: *Hold to select* (buttons work only after being held, with a progress bar)
and *Ignore repeated presses* help with tremors and accidental taps.

**Settings › Display**: text size, labels above/below/hidden, dark theme, high contrast.

**Settings › Switch scanning**: for people who use switches, see [switch-access.md](switch-access.md).

## 6. Keep a backup

**Settings › Boards › Back up all boards** saves everything as an `.obz` file (Open Board Format).
Keep a copy somewhere safe (a computer, cloud storage, email to yourself). It can be imported back
into Saywell, or into other AAC apps that read the Open Board Format.

## Tips

- Model: tap the words yourself while you talk, without expecting the person to copy.
- Start with a few motivating words and add more over time; hide what's not needed yet.
- *Settings › Talking › Go back home after each word* suits people who visit a topic board for one word.
- *Settings › Safety › Lock editing and settings* stays on unless the person edits their own boards.
